# EnergyTrading

A decentralized renewable energy credit trading and certificate generation platform.

## Overview

EnergyTrading is a blockchain-based platform that enables:
- Producers to register renewable energy production to a shared grid
- Automatic certificate generation based on production duration
- Fair distribution of certificates based on energy proportion

## Features

- **Energy Registration**: Secure recording of renewable energy production in a managed grid
- **Certificate Generation**: Automatic renewable energy certificate calculation over time
- **Proportional Distribution**: Certificates distributed based on production size
- **Grid Regulation**: Regulator oversight for grid integrity

## Functions

- `activate-grid`: Initialize the system with a grid regulator
- `register-production`: Add renewable energy production to the certificate-generating grid
- `issue-certificates`: Calculate and distribute renewable energy certificates to the pool
- `redeem-certificates`: Allow producers to claim their production value plus earned certificates

## Getting Started

1. Deploy the contract to your blockchain
2. Activate the grid with a trusted regulator
3. Producers can begin registering their renewable energy production
4. Periodically issue certificates to maintain accurate rewards

## Security

The system includes permission controls to ensure only authorized regulators can perform sensitive operations like certificate issuance.

## Use Cases

- Renewable energy certificate trading
- Carbon credit marketplaces
- Sustainable energy incentive programs
- Community solar and wind projects
